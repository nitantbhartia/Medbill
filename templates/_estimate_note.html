How these numbers work

Estimated charge is a typical U.S. hospital list price for the procedure, adjusted by a state cost index. It isn’t from any one hospital’s price list, and what you’re billed depends on the hospital, your insurance and what was done.

Medicare surgeon fee is what Medicare pays the doctor for the procedure code under its physician fee schedule. Medicare pays the hospital separately for the facility, so the two aren’t directly comparable.

To see what your own hospital charged, check your itemized bill.
