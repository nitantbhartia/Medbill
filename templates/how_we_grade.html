How BillKarma grades hospital prices

Each hospital's grade compares what it charges with what Medicare pays for the same services.

The data

Prices come from each hospital's own public price file and from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) {{ data_year }} Medicare rates. A hospital is only graded when at least {{ min_procedures }} of its procedures could be compared. With fewer, we show no grade.

The formula

For each compared procedure we divide the hospital's charge by the Medicare rate, then average across procedures. The average is the hospital's markup over Medicare.

What a grade does not say

A grade is about list prices, not the quality of care, and not what any one patient pays after insurance. Estimates only. Not legal or medical advice.
