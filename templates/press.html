Data requests

We can pull list prices for a procedure, a metro area or a hospital system from the price files we hold, with the method. Tell us the story and deadline.

Method in one paragraph

We read the machine-readable price files hospitals publish under the federal price transparency rule, keep one list price per hospital per billing code, and only publish a figure when enough hospitals report it. Medicare rates come from CMS fee schedules.

What we are

BillKarma checks hospital bills against Medicare rates and sends written disputes for a flat fee. We’re not a law firm and don’t give legal advice.
