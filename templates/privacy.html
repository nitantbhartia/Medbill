Privacy

What we read from your bill, what we save, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted.

When you check a bill

The file
An AI service (Google Gemini) reads your photo or PDF. We do not save the file itself.
What we save
The hospital name, each charge with its code and amount, the bill date and totals, the zip code you give, and the last four digits of the account number.
What we don’t save
The patient name on the bill, or the full account number.
Your email
Only if you enter it, to send you the report. We don’t send marketing email or share your address.

When you ask us to send a dispute

Your details
Your name, email, mailing address, the account number, and the billing office’s email or fax number. Income and household size if you give them, used only for a financial assistance request.
Your signatures
For each document you sign we record the time, your IP address and your browser, as proof of the signature.
The letter
We send the dispute letter and follow-ups to the hospital’s billing office, and keep a copy and any replies on your dispute page.
Payment
Stripe takes the payment. We never see or store your card number.

Other companies that handle it

Google Gemini
Reads the uploaded bill.
Stripe
Takes payments.
Resend
Delivers email, including the letter to the hospital.
Phaxio and Lob
Deliver a fax or certified letter, when one is sent.
Google Analytics
Counts page visits. It does not receive your bill.

Cookies

One cookie ties your browser to the bills you uploaded, so only you can open their results. It lasts {{ session_days }} days. A second cookie is set if you arrive through a referral link. Google Analytics sets its own.

The BillKarma iPhone app

The app sends the photos or PDF you choose to our servers to read and check your bill, with your ZIP code. It stores a random device ID (in your iCloud Keychain, so a new iPhone keeps your bills) instead of an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple, we also keep your Apple ID identifier and email address (which can be Apple’s private relay address) so your bills follow you across devices and we can email you a letter copy. Purchases are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, which receives your device ID and purchase records to confirm what you bought. The app has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

The app gives you a private email address for forwarding bills. An email you send to it, and the bill attached, are read and checked the same way as a bill you scan, and the result is saved with your other bills.

“Help improve BillKarma” is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, we keep what your insurer allowed for each billing code on an insurance statement you add, and how long a billing office kept a call on hold, without your name, member ID or claim number, to improve results for everyone. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

The financial assistance check works out your household income on your phone. Your income is never sent to us. Only the resulting percentage of the federal poverty level and your household size are sent, so we can show what applies to you.

If you use “Wait on hold for me”, we collect your phone number so we can text you a code and ring you when a person answers. Our phone provider, Twilio, handles the call and the text. We keep a one-way hash of your number to remember that you verified it, and we replace the numbers on a call with hashes when the call ends. We do not record the call or write down what is said, and after a person answers we stop listening altogether. The app also records which screens you use, tied to the random device ID, to fix problems and measure what works; it is never used to track you across other apps.

In the app, Settings → Delete my data removes every bill and report from our servers, and Delete my account and data also removes your account and revokes Sign in with Apple. Purchases stay with your Apple ID.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We do not yet delete saved bills on a schedule. To have your bill and details deleted, email support@billkarma.app from the address you used, or tell us the hospital and bill date.
