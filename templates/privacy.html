Privacy

What we read from your bill, what we save, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted.

When you check a bill

The file
An AI service (Google Gemini) reads your photo or PDF. We do not save the file itself.
What we save
The hospital name, each charge with its code and amount, the bill date and totals, the zip code you give, and the last four digits of the account number.
What we don’t save
The patient name on the bill, or the full account number.
Your email
Only if you enter it, to send you the report. We don’t send marketing email or share your address.
{% if not config.SLIM_LAUNCH %}

When you ask us to send a dispute

Your details
Your name, email, mailing address, the account number, and the billing office’s email or fax number. Income and household size if you give them, used only for a financial assistance request.
Your signatures
For each document you sign we record the time, your IP address and your browser, as proof of the signature.
The letter
We send the dispute letter and follow-ups to the hospital’s billing office, and keep a copy and any replies on your dispute page.
Payment
Stripe takes the payment. We never see or store your card number.
{% endif %}

Other companies that handle it

Google Gemini
Reads the uploaded bill.
Stripe
Takes payments.
Resend
Delivers email, including the letter to the hospital.
Phaxio and Lob
Deliver a fax or certified letter, when one is sent.
Google Analytics
Counts page visits. It does not receive your bill.

Cookies

One cookie ties your browser to the bills you uploaded, so only you can open their results. It lasts {{ session_days }} days. A second cookie is set if you arrive through a referral link. Google Analytics sets its own.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We do not yet delete saved bills on a schedule. To have your bill and details deleted, email support@billkarma.app from the address you used, or tell us the hospital and bill date.
