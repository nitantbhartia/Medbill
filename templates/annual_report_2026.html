Method

We collect the machine-readable price files hospitals publish under the federal price transparency rule and keep one list price (gross charge) per hospital per billing code, from its most recent file. Figures are shown only when at least {{ config.REPORT_MIN_HOSPITALS }} hospitals report the code. Data year {{ coverage.data_year or "2026" }}.

What list prices are

A list price is the hospital’s own starting price. Insurers usually pay negotiated rates below it; uninsured patients are often billed close to it. That’s why the spread between hospitals matters to patients.
