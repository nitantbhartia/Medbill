BillKarma works before, during, and after your hospital visit

Before Care
Know what you're walking into

Every U.S. hospital has a billing grade based on how much they charge compared to what Medicare pays for the same procedures. An F-grade hospital charges 8x Medicare or more. An A-grade charges 2x or less. Check yours before you schedule anything non-emergency.

BillKarma analyzes pricing data from every U.S. hospital

We download and parse the CMS-mandated price transparency files that every U.S. hospital is required to post. We compare each hospital's gross charges to the 2026 Medicare Outpatient Prospective Payment System (OPPS) rates for the same CPT codes. The result is a markup ratio and a billing grade for every hospital in America.

Grades are updated when hospitals post new price files. Approximately 28% of hospitals post files too incomplete to grade — we flag these as ungraded rather than assigning a score we can't defend.
