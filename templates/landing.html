Methodology

How we grade pricing fairness

Hospitals are required to publish prices, but they hide them in complex files. We compare their cash prices against Medicare rates.

  • Grade A: 2x Medicare or less (lowest markup)
  • Grade B: 2.1x to 3x Medicare (fair range)
  • Grade C: 3.1x to 5x Medicare (elevated)
  • Grade D: 5.1x to 8x Medicare (aggressive)
  • Grade F: Above 8x Medicare (highest-risk pricing)
