Questions

Do I need my itemized bill?

Yes. A summary bill shows totals, not the billing codes we compare. Hospitals must give you the itemized version on request; here’s how to ask.

Why compare with Medicare?

Medicare publishes what it pays for each billing code, so it’s a public, consistent yardstick. Hospitals aren’t required to charge Medicare rates, but a charge many times higher is worth questioning.

What happens after I pay for a dispute?

We send the letter to the hospital’s billing office, follow up on days {{ followup_days | join(", ") }} and show every reply on your dispute page. If the bill isn’t reduced within {{ config.DISPUTE_MAX_DAYS }} days, we refund the fee.

Does this work if I have insurance?

Yes, for what the hospital billed you. If your insurer denied the claim, that’s an appeal to the insurer, which we don’t handle; see appeal a denied claim.

Where does the data come from?

Medicare rates from CMS fee schedules, hospital prices from hospitals’ own price transparency files, and hospital contact details from CMS. How we grade hospitals
