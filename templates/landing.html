Questions

What happens to the bill I upload?

An AI service (Google Gemini) reads your photo or PDF. We do not save the file itself, or the patient name on it. We keep what was read: the hospital name, each charge with its code and amount, and the last four digits of the account number. We do not yet delete saved bills on a schedule; to have yours deleted, email support@billkarma.app.

Do I need my itemized bill?

Yes. A summary bill shows totals, not the billing codes we compare. Hospitals must give you the itemized version on request; here’s how to ask.

Why compare with Medicare?

Medicare publishes what it pays for each billing code, so it’s a public, consistent yardstick. Hospitals aren’t required to charge Medicare rates, but a charge many times higher is worth questioning. For scale, RAND found private insurers paid hospitals about 254% of Medicare rates in 2022. Hospital markups

What do I get for ${{ report_price }}?

Every flagged charge with the Medicare rate and the reason it was flagged, a dispute letter filled in for the charges you pick, and a phone script for the billing office. It’s one payment for one bill, with no subscription.

What if you find nothing wrong?

Then there’s nothing to unlock and you pay nothing. You only see the ${{ report_price }} option when we flag at least one charge.

Do you contact the hospital for me?

No. The letter and script are yours; you decide whether and how to send them.

Does this work if I have insurance?

Yes, for what the hospital billed you. If your insurer denied the claim, that’s an appeal to the insurer, which we don’t handle; see how to appeal a denial.
