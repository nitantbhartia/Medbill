Before you pay, do these three things

They work at almost any hospital, take less than an hour, and you can do them even after a bill has gone to collections.

  1. 01

    Ask for an itemized bill

    A summary bill hides the line items. You have a right to see every charge and its billing code.

    Get the request letter
  2. 02

    Ask about financial assistance

    Nonprofit hospitals must publish a financial assistance policy. Depending on income, the bill can be reduced or forgiven.

    Check if you qualify
  3. 03

    Check the charges

    Look for duplicate lines and prices far above what Medicare pays for the same code. Then dispute them in writing.

    Upload your bill

Already in collections?

You can ask the collector to prove the debt is yours and the amount is right. Send a validation letter within 30 days of their first notice.
