What we don’t do

  • Phone negotiation. Everything is in writing, so you keep a dated record.
  • Insurance denials. That’s a fight with your insurer, not the hospital. Start with how to appeal a denial.
  • Legal help. Sued or facing wage garnishment? Contact local legal aid or a consumer attorney.
