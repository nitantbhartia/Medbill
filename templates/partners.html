Reach people when they hold a hospital bill

Nonprofits, legal aid groups, credit unions, employer benefits teams and patient navigators often meet people right after a bill arrives. BillKarma checks that bill against what Medicare pays and sends a written dispute for the charges that look wrong.

Who it is for

Medical-debt nonprofits, legal aid offices, credit unions, employer benefits teams and patient navigators whose clients or members hold a hospital or facility bill they think is too high.

What a partner gets

  • A free scan link with your organization’s name on it, for example {{ config.APP_URL }}/scan?ref=your-org.
  • A monthly count of scans that came through your link.

That is all we promise. The scan itself is free for the person checking the bill.

How to start

  1. Email {{ config.PARTNER_CONTACT_EMAIL }} with your organization’s name and who you serve.
  2. Choose a short code for your link: lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, up to 40 characters.
  3. Share the link where your clients or members will see it.

What BillKarma does and does not do

What we do

Compare each charge on a hospital bill to the Medicare rate for that code, flag possible duplicates and unbundling, and, for a flat fee, send a written dispute to the hospital billing office and follow up on a fixed schedule.

What we do not do

  • We do not negotiate by phone.
  • We do not guarantee a reduction. The hospital decides the outcome.
  • We do not give legal advice. For a lawsuit, wage garnishment or bankruptcy, point people to a consumer attorney or legal aid.
