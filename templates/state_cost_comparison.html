Methodology

State cost rankings use CMS Geographic Practice Cost Index (GPCI) work and practice expense factors for 2026. These multipliers are applied to national average hospital chargemaster prices to estimate state-level costs. The national average for {{ anchor_procedure }} (${{ "{:,}".format(national_avg) }}) is derived from CMS OPPS data and voluntary hospital price transparency filings. State estimates are approximations — actual charges vary by facility, payer, and negotiated contract. Medicare pays a fixed national rate regardless of state; the cost index reflects commercial and self-pay prices.

Sources: CMS Physician Fee Schedule 2026 · CMS IPPS 2026
