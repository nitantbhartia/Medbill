Do You Qualify for Hospital Financial Assistance?

Most nonprofit hospitals must forgive bills for patients under 200-400% of the Federal Poverty Level. Many people qualify and never apply.

Your total household income before taxes. Use your most recent tax return as a guide.

How does hospital financial assistance work?

  • IRS Section 501(r) requires nonprofit hospitals to offer Financial Assistance Programs (FAPs) to maintain tax-exempt status.
  • Hospitals must make these programs widely available and cannot deny care to patients who qualify.
  • Eligibility is based on household income relative to the Federal Poverty Level.
  • You can apply retroactively — even after a bill has gone to collections.
