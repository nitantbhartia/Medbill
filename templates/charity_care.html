How does hospital financial assistance work?

  • Under IRS Section 501(r), nonprofit hospitals must offer Financial Assistance Programs (FAPs) to maintain their tax-exempt status. This is federal law, not charity.
  • Hospitals must publicize their FAP, provide free applications, and cannot use aggressive collection tactics against patients who may qualify.
  • Eligibility is based on your household income relative to the Federal Poverty Level (FPL). Most programs cover patients up to 200-400% of FPL.
  • You can apply retroactively — even after a bill has gone to collections.
