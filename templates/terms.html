Terms of use

Checking a bill is free and needs no agreement. These terms cover the Bill Check Report.

The Bill Check Report

The report is a one-time digital purchase for one bill. It shows each charge we flagged and why, and includes a dispute letter and phone script filled in with your bill’s details. You receive it on screen as soon as payment is confirmed.

What we are and aren’t

BillKarma is not a law firm, and nothing here is legal or financial advice. We don’t send anything to the hospital for you; you decide whether and how to use the letter and script. We can’t promise a reduction, because the hospital decides.

Accuracy

Findings are estimates based on Medicare rates, public pricing data and common billing rules. They can be wrong or incomplete, so check them against your own records before you rely on them.

The iPhone app

In the app, the report unlock and BillKarma Plus are digital purchases processed by Apple. A report unlock covers one bill. BillKarma Plus renews yearly until you cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, and unlocks every bill you scan while it is active. Bills you unlocked stay unlocked. Apple handles billing and refund requests (reportaproblem.apple.com). Signing in with Apple is optional. Clean bills are always free.

Problems with a purchase

Email support@billkarma.app and we’ll help. See also what we collect and keep.
