After your state's deadline, collectors can't win a lawsuit. Pick your state to see your exact rights.

The statute of limitations (SOL) is the deadline for a creditor to sue you over unpaid medical debt. Once it expires, the debt is time-barred — you can raise it as a complete defense in court. But be careful: making any payment or acknowledging the debt in writing can restart the clock from zero.
