How does hospital charity care work?

  • IRS Section 501(r) requires nonprofit hospitals to offer Financial Assistance Programs (FAPs) to keep their tax-exempt status. It's federal law, not optional charity.
  • Eligibility is based on household income relative to the Federal Poverty Level (FPL). Most programs cover patients up to 200–400% of FPL.
  • You can apply retroactively — even after a bill has gone to collections.
  • The hospital must suspend collection activity while your application is under review.
