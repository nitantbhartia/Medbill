Use BillKarma in ChatGPT or Claude

Add BillKarma to your AI assistant and ask about a hospital bill in plain words. It answers with what Medicare pays for the same code, the hospital’s billing office and a request letter when you need one.

No sign-in or API key. Nothing you send through it is stored.

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. Open Settings, then Apps & Connectors, then Advanced, and turn on Developer mode. It’s available on paid plans.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create.
  3. Name it BillKarma, paste the connector URL, and choose No authentication.
  4. In a chat, add BillKarma from the + menu, then ask your question.

Add it to Claude

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it BillKarma, paste the connector URL and choose Add.
  4. In a chat, turn BillKarma on from the + menu under Connectors.

On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner adds it in organization settings first. In Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http billkarma {{ mcp_url }}

Things to ask

  • “My ER bill charged $4,500 for code 99285. Is that too high?”
  • “What’s the billing phone number for Tampa General Hospital?”
  • “I only have a summary bill. Write me a request for the itemized one.”
  • “What would BillKarma charge to dispute a $12,000 bill?”
