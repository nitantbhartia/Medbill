About BillKarma

BillKarma checks hospital bills against what Medicare pays and shows you the charges that look wrong, with a dispute letter and phone script you send yourself. It is not affiliated with Credit Karma or any credit-score or personal-finance app.

Why it exists

Hospital billing departments know what each code normally pays. Patients usually don’t. BillKarma puts the public numbers next to your bill, for free, so you can see which charges are worth questioning without hiring a billing advocate or an attorney.

Where the data comes from

Hospital prices
The machine-readable price files hospitals publish under the federal Hospital Price Transparency Rule (45 CFR Part 180). Updated annually.
Medicare rates
The CMS Medicare Physician Fee Schedule and Outpatient Prospective Payment System. Both are public.
Billing rules
CMS National Correct Coding Initiative (NCCI) edits, AMA CPT guidelines and ICD-10-CM coding rules, used to flag likely billing errors.

How guides are written

Guides are checked against CMS.gov, KFF.org and AMA CPT sources before publication. Guides about patient rights cite the federal law involved, such as the No Surprises Act, the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act and HIPAA. Data pages show the source and the year the data was collected.

We correct errors when we find them. To report one, email corrections@billkarma.app.

Independence

BillKarma is independent. We don’t accept advertising from hospitals, insurers or billing companies, and we are not affiliated with CMS, Medicare or any government agency. We are not a law firm, and nothing here is legal or financial advice.
