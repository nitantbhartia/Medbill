# US Independent Medical Billing / Patient Advocate Market Size

Method note (2026-11-16): WebFetch was blocked in this session for the primary sites (pacboard.org, aphadvocates.org, gnanow.org, wikipedia.org returned DNS failures because they are outside the session's allowed network). All figures below come from search-result snippets of those pages, not full-page reads. Where a snippet gave no date, that is noted. Treat counts as directional until verified directly on the source pages.

## 1. How many Board Certified Patient Advocates (BCPA) exist? Growth trend?

### Takeaway
PACB's last public cumulative count I could find is 1,040 BCPAs (spring 2022). Growth went 149 (2018) to 893 (Apr 2021) to about 1,000 (fall 2021) to 1,040 (May 2022), so it slowed after the first rush. No 2023-2026 official total was found. A plausible 2026 figure is about 1,100-1,400, but that is an extrapolation.

### Cited Findings
- May 2018: the first cohort certified, with 149 BCPAs. — [PACB, 2018-05-03](https://www.pacboard.org/2018/05/03/patient-advocate-certification-board-certifies-first-cohort-of-board-certified-patient-advocates/); [Mercer](https://www.mercer.com/en-us/insights/us-health-news/michael-garrett-joins-first-cohort-of-board-certified-patient-advocates/)
- April 2021: cumulative total of 893 BCPAs. — [PACB Spring '21 cohort, 2021-04-13](https://www.pacboard.org/2021/04/13/spring21/)
- October 2021: total "just shy of 1000." — [PACB Fall 2021 cohort, 2021-10-28](https://www.pacboard.org/2021/10/28/pacb-announces-fall-2021-cohort-of-bcpas/)
- May 2022: "total number of individuals who have earned the BCPA" is 1,040. — [PACB Spring 2022 results, 2022-05-27](https://www.pacboard.org/2022/05/27/bcpa-exam-results-spring-2022/)
- APHA says there are "approximately 1,000" BCPAs (undated). — [APHA profession overview](https://aphadvocates.org/profession-overview/)
- A third-party guide says "more than 1,000" advocates hold the BCPA (undated). — [National Patient Advocacy Authority](https://nationalpatientadvocacyauthority.com/patient-advocate-certification-and-credentials.html)

### Inferences
- The step from about 1,000 (Oct 2021) to 1,040 (May 2022) is only about 40 in one exam window. The 2018-2021 surge looks like pent-up demand that has since tapered.
- BCPA is a credential, not a business count. Many holders are hospital, insurer or nonprofit employees, nurses and social workers, not independent practitioners. It also includes some non-US holders, so it overstates the number of independent US businesses.
- The credential is general patient advocacy. It is not billing-specific.

### Gaps
- No PACB total for 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 surfaced. A search for 2024 PACB exam results returned only unrelated boards. The PACB public directory (pacboard.org) would allow a direct count but could not be fetched.
- No data on the share of BCPAs who are US-based, independent, or who have lapsed or failed to recertify.

## 2. Membership counts: ACAP, GNA, NAHAC, AdvoConnection, APHA

### Takeaway
Most of these organizations do not publish membership numbers. The best counts are below. The largest, the GNA directory with roughly 1,000+ profiles, is a free listing and inflates the count of real businesses.

| Organization | Best available figure | Date |
|---|---|---|
| GNA (Greater National Advocates), gnanow.org, 501(c)(3), free directory | "More than 1,000 patient advocate profiles"; 2025 activity of about 200,000 unique searches and 120,000+ calls facilitated | 2025-2026 article |
| APHA (Alliance of Professional Health Advocates) | About 600 members | 2018 |
| AdvoConnection directory (APHA-run) | About 30 at launch (2011), just over 150 (2014), 224 (end of 2016) | 2011-2016 |
| ACAP (Alliance of Claims Assistance Professionals), claims.org, billing/claims specific | "Members in 16 states, just 33 total" | Undated, likely old |
| NAHAC (National Association of Healthcare Advocacy) | No count found; $175/yr dues listed by a third party | Undated |

### Cited Findings
- GNA's national directory "features more than 1,000 patient advocate profiles." It recorded about 200,000 unique directory searches and facilitated more than 120,000 calls to advocates in 2025. — [Middletown Life Magazine article (2026)](https://lifestyle.middletownlifemagazine.com/story/808245/no-one-should-navigate-healthcare-alone-not-even-patient-advocates/)
- GNA calls its directory "the nation's largest directory of Independent Patient Advocates." Listing is free for advocates, who must agree to the Collaborative Code of Ethics. — [GNA professionals page](https://gnanow.org/professionals-join-here.html); [GNA home](https://gnanow.org/)
- Search results show GNA directory pagination reaching at least page 39, and individual profiles show "member since" dates in 2025 and early 2026. — [gnanow.org/advocates?page=39](https://gnanow.org/advocates?page=39)
- GNA has an "Insurance & Billing" specialty filter. Listed billing advocates include Goodbill (Seattle), MedBillsAssist (Stamford, CT) and others. — [GNA Insurance & Billing advocates](https://gnanow.org/advocates?search_type=speciality_id&speciality_id=16); [GNA Seattle](https://gnanow.org/advocates/group-seattle)
- GNA staff estimates conflict across data aggregators: 17 employees (ZoomInfo), 11-50, and 2-10 (LinkedIn). — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/pic/greater-national-advocates/549393946); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/greater-national-advocates)
- APHA: about 600 members overall, as of 2018. — [APHA blog, "Successes, Failures, and My Biggest Surprise," 2018-10-23](https://aphablog.com/2018/10/23/successes-failures-and-my-biggest-surprise/)
- AdvoConnection directory: about 30 listed at launch, just over 150 private independent advocates by 2014, and 224 listed at the end of 2016. — [APHA blog 2014](https://aphablog.com/2014/01/26/all-dressed-up-with-big-places-to-go/); [APHA blog 2017 State of Patient Advocacy](https://aphablog.com/2017/01/02/2017-state-of-patient-advocacy-and-the-alliance-of-professional-health-advocates/)
- In 2018, AdvoConnection changed its admission rules for the directory. — [APHA blog 2018-04-09](https://aphablog.com/2018/04/09/announcing-a-big-change-for-admission-to-the-advoconnection-directory/)
- ACAP's membership roster "listed members in 16 states, just 33 total" (undated article). Its members are independently owned, fee-based, for-profit businesses that go through a vetting process. — [Jim Kendall, "De-mystifying Health Bills," hosted on claims.org](https://claims.org/pdf/De_mystifying_health_bills.pdf); [Daily Herald](https://e-edition.dailyherald.com/popovers/article_popover.aspx?guid=b1567866-4771-4f31-9ccf-6bae4992dd2b); [claims.org](https://www.claims.org/)
- NAHAC: no membership count found. A third-party listing shows $175 annual dues, with membership open to individuals, organizations, researchers and clinicians. — [healthadvocateprograms.com](https://healthadvocateprograms.com/listings/organizations/nahac.htm); [NAHAC about](https://nahac.com/about-nahac)

### Inferences
- These directories overlap heavily. Many advocates list in GNA, AdvoConnection and PACB at once, so the counts cannot be summed.
- GNA's 1,000+ profiles is likely the closest available proxy for "independent advocates who are actively marketing to consumers" in 2025-2026. Because listing is free, it includes part-timers and dormant listings.
- ACAP is the only billing/claims-specific association, and it is tiny (dozens of members). Billing-focused advocacy is a fragmented solo-practitioner niche.

### Gaps
- No 2022-2026 membership figures for APHA, AdvoConnection, ACAP or NAHAC. Their pages could not be fetched, and search snippets carried no counts.
- The date of the ACAP "33 members" figure is unknown.
- GNA's 990 filing or annual report would give program data, but was not retrieved.

## 3. Industry estimates of total independent health/billing advocates in the US

### Takeaway
There is no official or BLS count, because the field is unlicensed. Trisha Torrey/APHA estimates have ranged from 150-200 full-time private practices (2012) to 300-500 independent practices (2018) to "500 or more" privately paid advocates (APHA, undated, around 2020). Combined with GNA's 1,000+ listings, a defensible 2025-2026 range is about 500-1,500 independent US advocates. Perhaps a few hundred of these are full-time businesses.

### Cited Findings
- 2012 (Torrey): an estimated 150-200 in private practice ("people in business for themselves, or working in small private practices"), plus another 250-300 working part-time or working elsewhere for the moment. — [APHA blog, "Just How Many Patient Advocates Are There?", 2012-04-16](https://aphablog.com/2012/04/16/just-how-many-patient-advocates-are-there/)
- 2015: "maybe, MAYBE 400 independent advocates working in the private sector" in the US and Canada. — [APHA blog 2015-02-16](https://aphablog.com/2015/02/16/head-to-head-toe-to-toe-and-who-are-the-big-winners/)
- 2015: the Chicago Tribune ("Private patient advocates a growing, yet costly, trend in health care") cited 250-300 advocates for hire in the US, attributed to Torrey. This figure is secondhand, via Wikipedia. — [Wikipedia, Trisha Torrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trisha_Torrey); [Wikipedia, Medical-bill advocacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical-bill_advocacy)
- 2018: 300-500 advocates run an independent practice. — [APHA blog 2018-10-23](https://aphablog.com/2018/10/23/successes-failures-and-my-biggest-surprise/)
- APHA profession overview (dated September 2020 per a search snippet): an "educated guesstimate" of 500 or more privately paid professional advocates in the US. — [APHA profession overview](https://aphadvocates.org/profession-overview/)
- There is no training or licensing requirement for advocates, so no official registry exists. — [Becker's ASC](https://www.beckersasc.com/asc-coding-billing-and-collections/patient-advocates-guide-through-confusion-of-medical-bills/)
- 2026: the American Bar Association published "Independent Advocacy in Context: Why Distinctions Matter in the Debate Over For-Profit Patient Advocates," which signals active regulatory and legal debate. Its content was not read. — [ABA Health eSource 2026](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/health_law/resources/esource/2026/independent-advocacy-in-context/)
- 2023: a CHCAO special report, "The Critical Role of Patient and Health Care Advocates," surveyed patients, advocates and others. Over 90% of those who had used an advocate rated the experience positive. No practice counts were seen. — [CHCAO](https://chcao.org/)
- The adjacent IBISWorld category, Medical Billing Services, has 30,252 employees (2024). This is provider-side RCM companies, NOT patient advocates, and is not comparable. — [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/employment/medical-billing-services/6341/)
- Fees run about $100-$500/hr or about 25-35% contingency on savings (range 15-50%). These are vendor and guide sources, not audited. — [Checkbook](https://www.checkbook.org/national/patient-advocates/); [Pinnie](https://www.pinnie.com/articles/how-much-does-a-medical-billing-advocate-cost); [Umbra Health Advocacy](https://umbrahealthadvocacy.com/patient-advocate-costs/)

### Inferences
- Full-time vs. part-time: Torrey's 2012 split was about 40% full-time private practice (150-200) and about 60% part-time or "between" (250-300). If that ratio still holds roughly against a 2025 pool of about 1,000 GNA listings, it implies about 300-500 full-time independent practices and about 500-700 part-time, side or dormant practitioners. This is an inference, not a measured figure.
- The trajectory of about 150-200 (2012) to 300-500 (2018) to 500+ (2020) to 1,000+ GNA listings (2025) suggests the market roughly doubled every 5-6 years. In absolute terms it stays very small: low thousands of practitioners at most.
- Most independent practices are solo or 2-3 person shops. APHA's own framing ("people in business for themselves, or working in small private practices") supports this.

### Gaps
- No 2022-2026 estimate from Torrey, APHA or PACB of total independent advocates was found.
- No survey measuring full-time vs. part-time status after 2012 was found.
- No BLS SOC code isolates patient or billing advocates.

## 4. Share specializing in medical billing/claims vs. care navigation

### Takeaway
No source quantifies this split. The qualitative evidence suggests billing/claims specialists are a minority within the advocate community. ACAP, the billing-specific group, has only dozens of members, while BCPA, GNA and APHA skew clinical/navigation (many nurses and social workers).

### Cited Findings
- APHA notes that "some advocates focus instead on the billing and claims aspects" of care, but gives no percentage. — [APHA profession overview](https://aphadvocates.org/profession-overview/)
- GNA offers a distinct "Insurance & Billing" specialty filter. The number of advocates under it could not be counted. — [GNA Insurance & Billing](https://gnanow.org/advocates?search_type=speciality_id&speciality_id=16)
- The first National Health and Patient Advocate Survey found independent advocates tend to be older, white, female and more highly educated. — [APHA blog, hospital advocates category](https://aphablog.com/category/hospital-patient-advocates/)
- ACAP (claims/billing specialists): 33 members across 16 states (undated). — [claims.org PDF](https://claims.org/pdf/De_mystifying_health_bills.pdf)

### Inferences
- A rough guess is that 15-30% of independent advocates offer billing/insurance as a primary specialty, and a larger share list it as one of several services. This is unverified. A count of the GNA Insurance & Billing filter against the full directory would make it defensible.

### Gaps
- No quantitative specialty breakdown was found. Recommended next step: allow gnanow.org and pacboard.org in network settings and count the directory listings by specialty filter.

## 5. Larger firms (10+ staff) in billing advocacy

### Takeaway
Only a handful of consumer-facing billing-advocacy firms reach 10+ staff, about 5-15 identifiable companies, most of them VC-seeded startups with $2-4M rounds. Separately, large employer-paid navigation/advocacy companies (Health Advocate, Accolade/Transcarent) serve tens of millions of members, but they sell to employers and plans, not to individuals.

### Cited Findings
- **Goodbill** (Seattle): launched in 2021, raised $3.4M in 2022 and $2M in 2024, expanded to self-funded health plans, and is free unless it finds savings. LinkedIn lists 11-50 employees. — [GeekWire 2022](https://www.geekwire.com/2022/the-darkest-most-bottomless-pit-in-healthcare-goodbill-raises-3-4m-to-tackle-medical-billing/); [GeekWire 2024](https://www.geekwire.com/2024/goodbill-raises-2m-and-expands-customer-base-to-unearth-and-challenge-medical-billing-errors/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/goodbill)
- **Resolve**: $3.3M seed in July 2022 (led by AlleyCorp). It claimed nearly $20M in medical debt relieved, with 60-65% average savings. — [BusinessWire 2022-07-18](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220718005136/en/Resolve-Raises-3.3M-in-Seed-Funding); [Forbes 2022-07-22](https://www.forbes.com/sites/debgordon/2022/07/22/medical-billing-startup-raises-33m-in-seed-funding-as-medical-debt-increases/)
- **CoPatient**: launched an online bill audit and appeal platform in 2012 on a contingency model. No current size data. — [CNBC 2012](https://www.cnbc.com/2012/10/01/copatient-launches-online-platform-to-audit-appeal-medical-bills-on-behalf-of-patients.html)
- **DisputeBills**: $400K angel round, contingency model (date not shown). **Better**: 2017, raised $1.1M. — [StartUp Beat](https://startupbeat.com/disputebills-debuts-new-medical-bill-reduction-platform-closes-400k-round-financing/21335/); [TechCrunch 2017](https://techcrunch.com/2017/06/28/medical-bill-advocacy-startup-better-pledges-all-of-its-revenue-towards-relieving-medical-debt/)
- **Accolade / Transcarent** (employer navigation and advocacy): Transcarent agreed in January 2025 to acquire Accolade for about $621M and closed on 2025-04-08. The combined company serves over 20M members and 1,700+ employer and health plan clients. — [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/transcarent-acquire-accolade-621-million-health-benefits-navigation/736798/); [Transcarent press release](https://transcarent.com/press-releases/transcarent-completes-merger-with-accolade)
- A growing number of employers offer patient advocacy as a benefit via vendors such as Health Advocate Inc. This claim is unsourced, from a guide. — [Checkbook](https://www.checkbook.org/national/patient-advocates/)
- ACAP itself is listed at 11-50 headcount on ZoomInfo and Crunchbase. This likely reflects aggregator error or members' staff, not the association. — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/alliance-of-claims-assistance-professionals/1294904)

### Inferences
- The consumer billing-advocacy segment has perhaps 5-15 firms with 10+ staff (Goodbill, Resolve, Medical Cost Advocate, CoPatient if still active, and a few others), against hundreds of solo or micro practices. This is an estimate. Size data for Medical Cost Advocate and Claimable was not found.
- Employer-paid advocacy (Health Advocate, Accolade/Transcarent, and similar) is a separate B2B market orders of magnitude larger by member count. Those firms employ in-house advocates rather than buying tools for independents.
- For a software-demand thesis, the addressable "independent advocate" buyer pool is roughly 500-1,500 practitioners, of whom perhaps 300-500 are full-time businesses. There are also the few dozen to 15 billing-focused firms with teams.

### Gaps
- No size or funding data was found for Claimable, Medical Cost Advocate, or the current status of CoPatient. No member or employee data was found for Health Advocate (ownership and current scale were not confirmed in searches).
- No 2023-2026 data on whether Resolve or Goodbill grew beyond their seed-stage headcounts.
