What ClaimCoach does with your letter

When you scan a denial letter (or an insurance statement), the pages are sent to our server and read by an AI model run by Google on our behalf, to find the insurer, claim number, service, reason and dates. We do not keep the photos. They are read in memory and dropped. [Owner: confirm this against the Google AI terms in use, including that the pages are not used to train models.]

What we keep

We keep what was read from your letter (for example insurer, claim number, the service, the reason, dates and the address to send the appeal to), the details you type in (your name, mailing address, phone, member ID if you enter it) and the appeal letter we generate, so your appeal is there when you come back. We keep this until you delete it, and for no more than 12 months.

No account, no ads, no tracking

ClaimCoach has no account. Your appeals are tied to an anonymous device identifier stored in your phone's keychain. We do not sell your data, show ads or use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. We record a few first-party usage events (such as "paywall viewed") that never include the content of your letter.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat (which confirms purchases for us). They receive purchase information, not your letter.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications on your phone. They never name your service or the reason for the denial.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete my data. This removes your appeals, letters and device record from our servers and your phone. A one-time appeal you paid for cannot be restored after deletion.

Health information

Denial letters can contain health information. We use it only to explain your denial and write your appeal. [Owner/lawyer: add state-law notices, for example Washington My Health My Data, and a contact for privacy requests.]
